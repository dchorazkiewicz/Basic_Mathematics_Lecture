## Task

Use the convention $r\ge0$ and $0\le\theta<2\pi$.

1. Convert the Cartesian points $(3,3\sqrt3)$, $(-4,0)$ and $(0,-5)$ to radial coordinates.
2. Convert the radial points $(4,\frac{\pi}{3})$, $(3,\pi)$ and $(2,\frac{3\pi}{2})$ to Cartesian coordinates.
3. Plot all six points.
4. For each conversion, draw or identify the right triangle that supports the formulas $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both conversion tables, the common coordinate diagram and the angle choices.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Convert each result back to its original coordinate description. For Cartesian points, also verify $r^2=x^2+y^2$.
