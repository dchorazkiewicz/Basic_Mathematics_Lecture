## Task

Consider

$$
r(\theta)=\frac{6}{1+\frac12\cos\theta}.
$$

1. Identify $p$ and $e$ and classify the conic.
2. Compute $r(0)$, $r(\frac{\pi}{2})$, $r(\pi)$ and $r(\frac{3\pi}{2})$.
3. Convert the four radial points to Cartesian coordinates with the focus at the origin.
4. Plot the four points and sketch the corresponding ellipse.
5. Identify the nearest and farthest of the four points from the focus.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the radius table, the Cartesian coordinates, the classification and the sketch with the focus marked.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

For every point, check that its Cartesian distance from the focus equals the computed value of $r(\theta)$.

</div>
