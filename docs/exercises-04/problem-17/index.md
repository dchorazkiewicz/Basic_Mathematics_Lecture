## Task

Fix the focus $F=(0,0)$ and the directrix $D:x=4$. Compare the three eccentricities

$$
e=0.5,\qquad e=1,\qquad e=1.5.
$$

1. For each value, determine $p=ed$ and write the radial equation.
2. Compute the radii at $\theta=0$ and $\theta=\frac{\pi}{2}$.
3. Compute the radius at $\theta=\pi$ whenever it is finite and nonnegative.
4. Draw the three focus-directrix constructions with the same focus and directrix.
5. Explain what changes when only $e$ changes and what geometric data remain fixed.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include a three-case table, three labelled diagrams and a comparison paragraph.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

For one calculated point in each regime, evaluate both $d(P,F)$ and $d(P,D)$ and verify that their ratio equals the prescribed eccentricity.
