## Task

Compare

$$
P_1(t)=(4\cos t,2\sin t),\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi,
$$

$$
P_2(t)=(4\cos 2t,2\sin 2t),\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi,
$$

and

$$
P_3(t)=(4\cos t^2,2\sin t^2),\qquad 0\le t\le\sqrt{2\pi}.
$$

1. Verify that all three maps take values on the same ellipse.
2. Compute the positions at five equally spaced values of each parameter interval.
3. Determine how many times each rule traverses the ellipse and in which direction.
4. Compare the ordering and spacing of the selected points.
5. Explain why none of the three rules is automatically a physically correct time law for an orbiting body.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include three position tables, one common trajectory diagram and a note separating path, parameter rule and physical time.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Your comparison must include at least one pair of equal parameter fractions that produces different geometric positions.

</div>
