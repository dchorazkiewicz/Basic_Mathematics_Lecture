## Task

Let the focus be $F=(0,0)$ and the directrix be the line $D:x=2$. Consider the points

$$
A=(1,0),\quad B=(0,2),\quad C=(0,-2),\quad E=(-3,4).
$$

1. Compute $d(P,F)$ and $d(P,D)$ for each point $P$ in the list.
2. Decide which points satisfy the parabola condition $d(P,F)=d(P,D)$.
3. Derive the Cartesian equation of the parabola from

$$
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=2-x.
$$

4. Verify the accepted points in the Cartesian equation.
5. Draw the focus, directrix, vertex and verified points.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include a distance table, the derivation, substitution checks and a labelled diagram.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

The distance to the directrix must be measured perpendicularly. Check that the derived parabola has vertex $(1,0)$ and opens away from the directrix.

</div>
