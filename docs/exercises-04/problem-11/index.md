## Task

Compare the three parametrizations

$$
P_1(t)=(3\cos t,3\sin t),\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi,
$$

$$
P_2(s)=(3\cos 2s,3\sin 2s),\qquad 0\le s\le\pi,
$$

$$
P_3(u)=(3\cos u,-3\sin u),\qquad 0\le u\le2\pi.
$$

1. Verify that each parametrization satisfies $x^2+y^2=9$.
2. Compute the points at the beginning, one quarter, one half, three quarters and the end of each parameter interval.
3. Determine whether each parametrization traverses the circle clockwise or counterclockwise.
4. Explain why all three have the same geometric image although their parameter rules differ.
5. Prepare one diagram showing the common circle and arrows indicating the traversal direction.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include three comparison tables, one common diagram and a paragraph separating geometric image, orientation and parameter interval.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Your claim of equal geometric image must use both the Cartesian equation and the coverage of the entire circle, not only several matching points.

</div>
