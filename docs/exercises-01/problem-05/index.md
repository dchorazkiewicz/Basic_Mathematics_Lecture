## Task

Let

$$
P=(1,2),\qquad Q=(x,6).
$$

The distance between the points is $5$.

1. Substitute the data into the distance formula.
2. Solve the resulting equation for $x$.
3. Plot both possible positions of $Q$.
4. For each solution, draw the corresponding right triangle.
5. Check both answers directly by recomputing the distance.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Show the equation, both solutions for $x$, one diagram containing both possible points, and two direct checks.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Explain geometrically why two values of $x$ occur.
