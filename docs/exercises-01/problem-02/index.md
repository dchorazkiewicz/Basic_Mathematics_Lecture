## Task

In the original coordinate system, let

$$
A=(5,3),\qquad B=(0,-1),\qquad C=(2,4).
$$

A second coordinate system has the same axis directions, orientation and unit length, but its origin is the point

$$
O'=(2,-1)
$$

in the original system.

1. Draw both coordinate systems on one diagram.
2. Find the coordinates of $A$, $B$ and $C$ in the new system.
3. A point has new coordinates $D'=(-1,2)$. Find its coordinates in the original system.
4. Check your answers by reading the horizontal and vertical displacements from $O'$.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include a two-system diagram and a table with columns: point, original coordinates, new coordinates.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

For every point, reconstruct its original coordinates by adding the old coordinates of $O'$ to its new coordinates.

</div>
