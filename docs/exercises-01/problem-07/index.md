## Task

Consider the two sets

$$
L:\ y=-x+2,
$$

and

$$
C:\ x^2+y^2=5.
$$

For each point below, decide whether it belongs to $L$, to $C$, to both sets, or to neither set:

$$
A=(0,2),\quad B=(1,1),\quad C_1=(2,0),\quad D=(-1,3),\quad E=(-1,-2).
$$

For every decision:

1. substitute the coordinates into the equation of $L$;
2. substitute the coordinates into the equation of $C$;
3. record the result in a classification table.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include one table with columns: point, test for $L$, test for $C$, final classification.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Plot both sets and all five points. Check that the geometric picture agrees with the algebraic classification.

</div>
