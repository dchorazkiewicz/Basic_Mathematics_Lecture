## Problem

Prepare a one-page mathematical note about the circle with centre $(1,-1)$ and radius $2$.

Your note must present the same geometric object in several connected forms.

1. Begin from the fixed-distance condition and derive the relation $(x-1)^2+(y+1)^2=4.$
2. Write two function formulas describing the upper and lower halves of the circle, including their domain.
3. Construct a parametrization of the entire circle.
4. Write an inequality describing the closed disk bounded by the circle.
5. Produce a table of at least six points generated from the parametrization.
6. Verify at least three of those points in the Cartesian relation.
7. Mark the centre, radius, generated points and both semicircles on one diagram.
8. Prepare a comparison table with the headings:
   - representation;
   - input data;
   - generated set;
   - limitation or advantage.
9. End with a short explanation of the statement:

   > An equation is not merely a calculation to perform; it can be an instruction generating geometry.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

The submitted note must contain:

- the distance derivation;
- the Cartesian relation;
- two function branches;
- one parametrization;
- the disk inequality;
- a point table;
- one annotated diagram;
- a representation-comparison table;
- a conclusion of five to eight sentences.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

If a graph, table or parametrization is produced with software or AI assistance, the solution must state what was generated and must independently verify at least three points and all displayed formulas.
