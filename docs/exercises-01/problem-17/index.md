## Problem

Consider two parametrizations:

$$
A:\quad x(t)=\cos t,\qquad y(t)=\sin t,\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi,
$$

and

$$
B:\quad x(s)=\cos(2s),\qquad y(s)=\sin(2s),\qquad 0\le s\le\pi.
$$

1. Verify algebraically that every point generated by either parametrization lies on $x^2+y^2=1.$
2. Complete parameter tables for $t=0,\frac{\pi}{2},\pi,\frac{3\pi}{2},2\pi$
   and $s=0,\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{3\pi}{4},\pi.$
3. Compare the corresponding generated points.
4. Explain why both parametrizations cover the same geometric circle.
5. Compare the parameter values associated with the point $(0,1)$.
6. Draw two sequences of labelled points showing how the parameter moves around the circle.
7. Explain what is different between the two descriptions even though the generated set is the same.
8. Modify one parametrization so that the circle is traversed in the opposite orientation and justify your modification.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both tables, one common circle diagram with two parameter labels, the algebraic verification and a comparison paragraph.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Your reverse-orientation parametrization must be checked at three parameter values, including the initial point.
