## Questions deliberately postponed {#later-questions}

At this point we can read a matrix, apply it to vectors, and interpret the result through the images of the basis vectors, the transformed grid, and transformed figures.

Several natural questions now appear:

- How does a matrix change area?
- Can two different input vectors produce the same output?
- When can an action be reversed?

These questions are important, but they are not part of this lecture. They require the ideas of determinant and invertibility and belong to **Lecture 07**.

Before that, **Lecture 06** will study what happens when one transformation is followed by another. Matrix multiplication will arise from composing two visible actions.

!!! note "Boundary of Lecture 05"
    Here we only calculate outputs and describe the visible action. We do not yet classify matrices as invertible or non-invertible, calculate determinants, or analyse loss of information.
