## What comes next? {#later-questions}

We can now read a matrix, apply it to vectors and interpret its action through basis vectors, grids and figures.

Three further questions arise naturally:

- How does a matrix change area?
- Can two different input vectors produce the same output?
- When can a transformation be reversed?

Lecture 06 first studies two transformations performed in succession. This leads to matrix multiplication.

Lecture 07 then introduces determinants and invertibility to answer the questions about area, information loss and reversibility.
