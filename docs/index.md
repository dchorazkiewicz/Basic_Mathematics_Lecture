# Basic Mathematics

*A course in analytical geometry, linear algebra and calculus.*

!!! note "Before the lectures"
    The introduction explains the purpose of the course, the weekly learning cycle, and the standard expected of mathematical notes.

    **[Read the course introduction →](introduction/purpose-and-philosophy.md)**

The course map contains two linked layers. **Lecture** opens the teaching material. **Exercises** opens the design page for the corresponding class work: what is transferred from the lecture, what students should develop, and what should later become a workbook problem set.

## Part I · Objects and Geometry {#course-map}

| Lecture | Topic | Scope | Lecture | Exercises |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Cartesian Geometry and Coordinates | Coordinates as representations, Euclidean distance, and equations generating sets of points. | [Open →](lecture-01/index.md) | [Design set 01 →](exercises-01/index.md) |
| **02** | Vectors, Bases, and Coordinate Systems | Vectors as changes, vector operations, dot products, projections, linear combinations and bases. | [Open →](lecture-02/index.md) | [Design set 02 →](exercises-02/index.md) |
| **03** | Lines and Planes | Objects generated by points and directions, equivalent representations, intersections and distance. | [Open →](lecture-03/index.md) | [Design set 03 →](exercises-03/index.md) |
| **04** | Conic Sections and Mathematical Models | Parametric curves, radial description, eccentricity, conic families and the distinction between shape and physical model. | [Open →](lecture-04/index.md) | [Design set 04 →](exercises-04/index.md) |

## Part II · Actions and Linear Algebra

| Lecture | Topic | Scope | Lecture | Exercises |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **05** | Matrices as Transformations | A matrix as one action on the plane, read through basis images, transformed grids and figures. | [Open →](lecture-05/index.md) | [Design set 05 →](exercises-05/index.md) |
| **06** | Composition of Transformations | Matrix multiplication arising from consecutive actions. | Planned | Planned |
| **07** | Determinants and Invertibility | Area scaling, loss of information and the possibility of reversing an action. | Planned | Planned |
| **08** | Linear Systems as Inverse Problems | Recovering unknown input from a known output in $Ax=b$. | Planned | Planned |

## Part III · Processes and Calculus

| Lecture | Topic | Scope | Lecture | Exercises |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **09** | Sequences and Asymptotic Behaviour | Discrete processes, monotonicity, boundedness and limits of sequences. | Planned | Planned |
| **10** | Limits and Continuity of Functions | Heine’s definition, local behaviour, $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ control and continuity. | Planned | Planned |
| **11** | Derivatives and Qualitative Analysis | Instantaneous change, local linearity, monotonicity and elementary function analysis. | Planned | Planned |
| **12** | Integration and Accumulation | Riemann sums, accumulation, antiderivatives and the fundamental theorem. | Planned | Planned |
