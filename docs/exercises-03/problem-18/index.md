## Task

Consider the two triples

$$
A=(0,0,0),\quad B=(1,2,1),\quad C=(2,4,2)
$$

and

$$
P=(0,0,0),\quad Q=(1,0,1),\quad R=(0,1,1).
$$

1. Compute $B-A$ and $C-A$.
2. Decide whether the first triple is collinear.
3. Compute $(B-A)\times(C-A)$ and interpret the result.
4. Compute $Q-P$ and $R-P$.
5. Compute $(Q-P)\times(R-P)$.
6. Use the nonzero cross product to write a plane through $P$, $Q$ and $R$.
7. Explain why three collinear points do not determine a unique plane, while three noncollinear points do.
8. Prepare one diagram comparing the two cases.

### Required record

Include both dependence analyses, both cross products, the plane equation and the comparison diagram.
