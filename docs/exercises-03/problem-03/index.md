## Task

Consider the line

$$
P(t)=(3,-1)+t(2,3).
$$

1. Give one normal vector $n$ perpendicular to the direction $(2,3)$.
2. Write the normal equation $n\cdot(P-P_0)=0$.
3. Expand it to an equation of the form $Ax+By=C$.
4. Check that the point $(3,-1)$ satisfies the implicit equation.
5. Generate two additional points from the parametric equation and check them in the implicit equation.
6. Draw the line together with its direction and normal vectors based at $P_0$.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the chosen normal vector, both equations, three substitution checks and the drawing.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Confirm explicitly that the dot product of the direction and normal vectors is zero.

</div>
