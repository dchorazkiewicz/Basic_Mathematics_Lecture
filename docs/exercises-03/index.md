# Exercise Set 03

The set follows the division intended for the later student workbook:

- **Problems 01–10 — required practice:** calculate, convert, draw and verify;
- **Problems 11–20 — developing work:** compare representations, justify constructions, explain geometry and prepare a mathematical note.

Each problem has its own Markdown file. Separate editable `solution.md` files will be added only in the forkable student workbook.

## Problems 01–10 — required practice {#required-practice}

<div class="problem-list" markdown>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-01/">
<strong>Problem 01 — Generating points of a line</strong>
<span>Use a parameter to generate points, test membership and connect the equation with a drawing.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-02/">
<strong>Problem 02 — Line through two points</strong>
<span>Construct two parametric descriptions from the same pair of points and verify their equivalence.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-03/">
<strong>Problem 03 — From a direction to a normal equation</strong>
<span>Convert one parametric line to normal and implicit forms and check generated points.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-04/">
<strong>Problem 04 — Slope, direction and a vertical line</strong>
<span>Interpret slope geometrically and compare ordinary and vertical-line representations.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-05/">
<strong>Problem 05 — Intersection of two lines</strong>
<span>Find one intersection algebraically, verify it and compare the result with the graph.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-06/">
<strong>Problem 06 — Classifying pairs of lines</strong>
<span>Distinguish intersecting, parallel and coincident lines from their directions and equations.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-07/">
<strong>Problem 07 — Distance from a point to a line</strong>
<span>Compute the distance, construct the perpendicular foot and verify orthogonality.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-08/">
<strong>Problem 08 — Generating points of a plane</strong>
<span>Use two parameters to generate and test points of a plane.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-09/">
<strong>Problem 09 — Normal vector of a plane</strong>
<span>Compute a normal with the cross product and derive a normal equation.</span>
</a>

<a class="problem-card problem-card--required" href="problem-10/">
<strong>Problem 10 — Plane through three points</strong>
<span>Construct parametric and normal descriptions and verify all original points.</span>
</a>

<div class="problem-list" markdown>
