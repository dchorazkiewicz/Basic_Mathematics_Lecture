## Task

Consider the line

$$
L:\quad 2x-y-3=0.
$$

Find and describe all points whose distance from $L$ is $\sqrt5$.

1. Identify a normal vector to $L$ and compute its length.
2. Use the point-line distance formula to derive the condition on $(x,y)$.
3. Show that the condition splits into two linear equations.
4. Write both resulting lines explicitly.
5. Explain geometrically why the solution consists of two parallel lines.
6. Draw the original line and both solution lines.
7. Choose one point from each solution line and verify its distance from $L$.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the derivation, both equations, the drawing and two direct distance checks.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Confirm that both solution lines are parallel to $L$ and lie on opposite sides of it.

</div>
