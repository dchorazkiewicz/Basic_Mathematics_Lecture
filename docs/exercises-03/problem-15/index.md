## Task

Let the line be $L:y=1$ and let $P=(2,5)$.

1. Find the perpendicular foot $Q$ of $P$ on $L$.
2. Compute the length $|PQ|$.
3. Choose three other points $R_1,R_2,R_3$ on $L$ and compute $|PR_i|$.
4. Draw all four connecting segments.
5. For a general point $R=(r,1)$ on the line, write $|PR|^2$ as a function of $r$.
6. Explain directly from this expression why the minimum occurs at the perpendicular foot.
7. Relate the calculation to the right triangle formed by $P$, $Q$ and any other point $R$ on the line.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the numerical comparison, the general squared-distance expression, the diagram and a short argument identifying the minimum.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Check that every chosen $R_i$ lies on the line and that each nonperpendicular segment is longer than $PQ$.

</div>
