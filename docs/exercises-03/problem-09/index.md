## Task

Let

$$
P_0=(1,2,0),\qquad u=(1,0,1),\qquad v=(0,2,-1).
$$

1. Compute $n=u\times v$.
2. Verify that $n\cdot u=0$ and $n\cdot v=0$.
3. Write the parametric equation $P=P_0+su+tv$.
4. Write the normal equation $n\cdot(P-P_0)=0$.
5. Expand it to the form $Ax+By+Cz=D$.
6. Generate three points from the parametric equation and check them in the normal equation.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the cross product, both orthogonality checks, both plane equations and three substitutions.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

The initial point $P_0$ and all generated points must satisfy the final normal equation.

</div>
