## Task

Consider

$$
L_1:\quad P(t)=(0,1)+t(2,1)
$$

and

$$
L_2:\quad P(s)=(4,0)+s(-1,2).
$$

1. Find the intersection by equating the two parametric descriptions.
2. Convert both lines to Cartesian equations.
3. Find the intersection again using the Cartesian equations.
4. Verify the resulting point in both original parametrizations.
5. Compare the two methods: identify which quantities are unknown and which equations are solved.
6. State one situation in which the parametric method is more natural and one in which the Cartesian method is shorter.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both complete solutions followed by a comparison table of the two methods.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Both methods must produce the same geometric point, and that point must be checked in both original lines.
