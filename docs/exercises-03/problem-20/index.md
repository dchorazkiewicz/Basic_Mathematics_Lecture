## Task

Prepare a concise decision guide for choosing a representation of a line or plane.

Your guide must address the following situations:

1. generating points from a known point and direction;
2. testing whether a point belongs to an object;
3. reading the slope of a nonvertical line;
4. finding the intersection of two lines;
5. computing the distance from a point to a line;
6. constructing a plane from one point and two directions;
7. constructing a plane through three noncollinear points;
8. finding the intersection of a line and a plane.

For each situation:

1. choose the representation you would use first;
2. write its generic form;
3. identify the geometric data contained in the formula;
4. give one small numerical example;
5. state how the result should be verified.

Finish with one diagram connecting the ideas

$$
\text{point and direction}\longrightarrow\text{parametric description}
$$

and

$$
\text{point and normal}\longrightarrow\text{normal description}.
$$

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Produce a structured mathematical note or flowchart containing all eight situations, generic formulas, examples and verification rules.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Every formula in the guide must label which expressions are points, vectors, parameters or real numbers.
