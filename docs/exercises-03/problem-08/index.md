## Task

Consider the plane

$$
P(s,t)=(1,0,2)+s(1,1,0)+t(0,1,1).
$$

1. Compute the points corresponding to $(s,t)=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(-1,2)$.
2. Record the results in a table.
3. Decide whether $A=(2,2,3)$ belongs to the plane and find suitable parameters if it does.
4. Decide whether $B=(2,0,3)$ belongs to the plane.
5. Explain what geometric role is played by the two direction vectors.
6. Prepare a labelled three-dimensional sketch or two labelled views of the plane.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the parameter table, both membership tests and the labelled visualization.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Substitute the recovered parameters for every point claimed to belong to the plane.
