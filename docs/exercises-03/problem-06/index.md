## Task

Classify each pair as intersecting, parallel and distinct, or coincident.

1. $2x-y=1$ and $4x-2y=7$.
2. $x+2y=3$ and $2x+4y=6$.
3. $x-y=0$ and $x+y=4$.
4. $3x+y=5$ and $6x+2y=10$.

For every pair:

1. identify direction or normal vectors;
2. compare their directions;
3. when appropriate, find the intersection point;
4. when the lines may be coincident, verify one common point;
5. draw a small sketch of the geometric situation.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Prepare a classification table containing the equations, direction comparison, final classification and any intersection point.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Every claim of coincidence must be supported by showing that one equation is a nonzero multiple of the other or by an equivalent set argument.

</div>
