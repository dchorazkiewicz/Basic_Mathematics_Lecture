## Task

Use the line through $P_0=(2,-1)$ with direction $v=(3,2)$.

1. Write its parametric equation.
2. Determine its slope and write the slope-intercept equation.
3. Choose a normal vector and write the normal equation.
4. Expand the normal equation to an implicit Cartesian equation.
5. Generate two points from the parametric equation and verify them in both Cartesian descriptions.
6. Draw one diagram containing the line, $P_0$, the direction vector, the normal vector and a slope triangle attached at $P_0$.
7. Prepare a short presentation card explaining what each representation makes easy to see.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

The final page must contain the three representations, one labelled diagram, two substitution checks and a comparison of their uses.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

All three descriptions must generate exactly the same two test points.

</div>
