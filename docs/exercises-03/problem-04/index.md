## Task

Consider the lines

$$
L_1:\quad P(t)=(-1,2)+t(3,1)
$$

and

$$
L_2:\quad x=4.
$$

1. Find the slope of $L_1$ from its direction vector.
2. Write $L_1$ in the form $y=mx+b$.
3. Draw a slope triangle attached to $L_1$ at the point $(-1,2)$.
4. Give a direction vector and a normal vector for $L_2$.
5. Write a parametric equation and a normal equation for $L_2$.
6. Explain in one sentence why $L_2$ cannot be written in the form $y=mx+b$.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both descriptions of $L_1$, both applicable descriptions of $L_2$ and one drawing showing the slope triangle.

</div>

### Verification
