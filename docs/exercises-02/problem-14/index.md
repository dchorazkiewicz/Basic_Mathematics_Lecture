## Problem

Fix the vector

$$
u=(2,0)
$$

and consider

$$
v_1=(3,1),\quad v_2=(0,3),\quad v_3=(-1,2),\quad v_4=(-3,0),\quad v_5=(1,-2).
$$

1. Draw all vectors from the origin.
2. Before calculating, predict the sign of $u\cdot v_i$ from the diagram.
3. Compute every dot product.
4. Classify each angle with $u$ as acute, right or obtuse.
5. Prepare a table containing the vector, visual prediction, dot product, sign and angle type.
6. Explain why the dot product is a measure of directional agreement rather than merely another coordinate formula.
7. Add one vector of your own for which the dot product with $u$ is zero and one for which it is negative.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the diagram, the prediction-and-verification table and a conclusion of five to seven sentences.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Every predicted sign must be compared with the computed result. Any incorrect prediction must be corrected and briefly explained.
