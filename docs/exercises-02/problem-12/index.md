## Problem

A point starts at

$$
P=(1,1).
$$

Consider the changes

$$
u=(4,1),\qquad v=(-1,3),\qquad w=(2,-2).
$$

1. Draw the route corresponding to $u$ followed by $v$.
2. Draw the route corresponding to $v$ followed by $u$.
3. Compare the two endpoints and explain geometrically what this shows about $u+v$ and $v+u$.
4. Draw the three-step routes $(u+v)+w$ and $u+(v+w)$.
5. Verify all endpoints by coordinate calculation.
6. Construct a third route, using at least three different vectors, with the same total displacement as $u+v$.
7. Explain what information the total vector preserves and what information about the route it discards.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include at least three route diagrams, endpoint calculations and a short comparison note.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Every route claimed to have the same displacement must begin at $P$ and end at the same point.

</div>
