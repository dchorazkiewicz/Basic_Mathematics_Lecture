## Task

A point starts at

$$
P=(2,-1)
$$

and undergoes the consecutive changes

$$
u=(3,2),\qquad v=(-1,4),\qquad w=(-2,-1).
$$

1. Find the point after applying $u$.
2. Find the point after subsequently applying $v$.
3. Find the final point after applying $w$.
4. Compute the total vector $u+v+w$.
5. Apply the total vector directly to $P$ and verify that the same final point is obtained.
6. Draw the three-step route and the single total displacement.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include the successive points, the combined vector and one route diagram.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

The endpoint of the broken-line route must agree with $P+(u+v+w)$.

</div>
