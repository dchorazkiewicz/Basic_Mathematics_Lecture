## Problem

Let

$$
u=(4,2),\qquad v=(1,1).
$$

1. Compute the coefficient
   $$
   c_v=\frac{u\cdot v}{v\cdot v}
   $$
   and the vector projection $c_vv$.
2. Repeat the calculation using the opposite direction $-v$.
3. Compare the two coefficients.
4. Compare the two projected vectors.
5. Draw $v$, $-v$, $u$ and the projected vector on one diagram.
6. Explain why reversing the chosen direction changes the coefficient but not the geometric projected vector.
7. State clearly which quantities depend on the chosen orientation of the direction line and which do not.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both calculations, one comparison table, one diagram and a conclusion of four to six sentences.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Confirm directly that

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{-v}u=\operatorname{proj}_v u.
$$
