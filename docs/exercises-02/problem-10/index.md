## Task

Let

$$
b_1=(1,1),\qquad b_2=(1,-1).
$$

For each vector

$$
u=(4,2),\qquad v=(-2,6),\qquad w=(5,-1),
$$

1. find coefficients $\alpha,\beta$ such that
$$
   u=\alpha b_1+\beta b_2
$$
   and similarly for $v$ and $w$;
2. write the coordinate vectors $[u]_B$, $[v]_B$ and $[w]_B$;
3. reconstruct each original vector from its basis coordinates;
4. compare the standard coordinates of each vector with its $B$-coordinates.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include a table containing standard coordinates, $B$-coordinates and a reconstruction check for all three vectors.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Every pair of basis coordinates must reproduce the original vector exactly.

</div>
