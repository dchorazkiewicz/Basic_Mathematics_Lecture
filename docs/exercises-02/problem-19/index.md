## Problem

Let

$$
u=(5,3)
$$

in the standard basis, and let

$$
b_1=(2,0),\qquad b_2=(1,1).
$$

1. Show that $b_1,b_2$ form a basis of the plane.
2. Find the coordinates $[u]_B$.
3. Reconstruct $u$ from those coordinates.
4. Draw the standard basis vectors, the basis $B$ and the vector $u$ on one diagram.
5. Represent the decomposition of $u$ in basis $B$ geometrically.
6. Compare the two coordinate pairs attached to the same vector.
7. Explain which object is unchanged and which data depend on the chosen basis.
8. Give a second vector whose coordinates in basis $B$ are $(2,-1)$ and verify the result.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include both coordinate descriptions, the reconstruction, one annotated diagram and a conclusion of five to seven sentences.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

The geometric endpoint of the vector must be the same when reconstructed from either coordinate description.

</div>
