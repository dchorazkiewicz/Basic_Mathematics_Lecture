## Problem

Let

$$
u=(2,1).
$$

For each scalar

$$
-2,\quad -1,\quad 0,\quad \frac12,\quad 1,\quad \frac32,
$$

1. compute the corresponding multiple of $u$;
2. draw all multiples from the origin on one diagram;
3. record the length of each multiple;
4. classify whether it has the same orientation as $u$, opposite orientation, or no direction;
5. prepare a table with the columns: scalar, vector, length, orientation;
6. formulate a general rule describing separately the effects of the sign and the absolute value of a scalar.

<div class="required-record" markdown>

### Required record

Include one annotated diagram, the complete table and a general rule stated in your own words.

</div>

<div class="verification-box" markdown>

### Verification

Check numerically that $\|au\|=|a|\,\|u\|$ for at least three nonzero scalars from the list.

</div>
