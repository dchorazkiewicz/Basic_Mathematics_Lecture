Problem 15 — Deriving the radial conic equation

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 04 Lecture 04

Task

Place the focus at the origin and let the directrix be \(D:x=d\), where \(d>0\). Assume the point \(P\) lies on the side of the directrix containing the focus and use \(r\ge0\).

  1. Draw a labelled diagram containing the focus, directrix, polar axis, angle \(\theta\), point \(P\) and perpendicular foot on the directrix.
  2. Show that the distance from \(P\) to the directrix is \(d-r\cos\theta\).
  3. Starting from \(d(P,F)=e\,d(P,D)\), derive
\[ r(1+e\cos\theta)=ed. \]
  1. Define \(p=ed\) and obtain \(r=\frac{p}{1+e\cos\theta}\).
  2. List every geometric or sign assumption used in the derivation.

Required record

Include the labelled diagram, the complete derivation and an explicit assumption list.

Verification

Choose numerical values of \(d\), \(e\) and \(\theta\), calculate the same point by both the distance condition and the final radial formula, and compare the results.
