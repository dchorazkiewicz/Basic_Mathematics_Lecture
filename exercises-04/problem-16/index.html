Problem 16 — Cartesian and focal descriptions of one ellipse

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 04 Lecture 04

Task

Consider the ellipse

\[ \frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1. \]
  1. Determine \(a\), \(b\), the focal distance \(c\) and the eccentricity \(e\).
  2. Choose the focus \(F=(4,0)\) as the pole and show that \(p=\frac{b^2}{a}=\frac95\).
  3. Write the focal radial equation with the nearest vertex in the direction \(\theta=0\).
  4. Compute \(r(0)\) and \(r(\pi)\) and identify the corresponding Cartesian vertices.
  5. Prepare a two-column comparison: information visible immediately in the Cartesian equation and information visible immediately in the focal radial equation.

Required record

Include the parameter calculations, both equations, the two focal distances, a labelled sketch and the comparison table.

Verification

Convert the two radial vertices back to global Cartesian coordinates using \(F=(4,0)\) and verify them in the original ellipse equation.
