Exercises · Lecture 07

Determinants and Invertibility

Damian Chorążkiewicz

Calculate determinants, interpret their signs and connect a zero determinant with collapse and information loss.

← Return to Lecture 07

Exercise Set 07

For each matrix, connect the calculation with the geometry:

\[ \det A \longleftrightarrow \text{signed area} \longleftrightarrow \text{column directions} \longleftrightarrow \text{information loss or reversibility}. \]

Core practice

Problem Task
01 Calculate the determinant of six simple \(2\times2\) matrices.
02 Match determinant values with transformed unit squares.
03 Draw the image of the unit square and read its ordinary area.
04 Compare a stretch and a shear with the same or different area factors.
05 Identify whether orientation is preserved or reversed.
06 Calculate the determinant of a reflection and explain the negative sign.
07 Find which matrices collapse the unit square to a segment.
08 For \(S=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}\), find four different inputs with the same output.
09 Find a nonzero vector sent to zero by a given singular matrix.
10 Decide whether a linear action can be reversed uniquely.
