Problem 07 — Distance from a point to a line

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 03 Lecture 03

Task

Let \(P=(3,1)\) and let the line be

\[ L:\quad 2x-y-4=0. \]
  1. Identify a normal vector to \(L\).
  2. Compute the distance from \(P\) to \(L\).
  3. Find the foot \(Q\) of the perpendicular from \(P\) to \(L\).
  4. Verify that \(Q\) belongs to \(L\).
  5. Verify that the vector \(P-Q\) is perpendicular to a direction vector of \(L\).
  6. Draw \(P\), \(Q\), the line and the perpendicular segment.

Required record

Include the distance calculation, coordinates of \(Q\), both verification steps and the drawing.

Verification

Confirm that the computed distance equals \(\|P-Q\|\).
