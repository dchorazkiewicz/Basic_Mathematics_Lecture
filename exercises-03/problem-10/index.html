Problem 10 — Plane through three points

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 03 Lecture 03

Task

Let

\[ A=(1,0,0),\qquad B=(0,1,0),\qquad C=(0,0,1). \]
  1. Compute the direction vectors \(u=B-A\) and \(v=C-A\).
  2. Check that \(u\) and \(v\) are not parallel.
  3. Write a parametric equation of the plane through \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\).
  4. Compute a normal vector using \(u\times v\).
  5. Write the normal equation and expand it to \(Ax+By+Cz=D\).
  6. Verify that all three points satisfy the final equation.
  7. Compute the distance from the origin to the plane.

Required record

Include the two directions, parametric equation, normal vector, normal equation, three point checks and the distance calculation.

Verification

Check both dot products \(n\cdot u\) and \(n\cdot v\) before accepting the normal vector.
