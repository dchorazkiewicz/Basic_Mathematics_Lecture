Problem 19 — Intersection of a line and a plane

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 03 Lecture 03

Task

Consider the line

\[ L:\quad P(t)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1) \]

and the plane

\[ \Pi:\quad x+y+z=6. \]
  1. Substitute the parametric line into the plane equation.
  2. Solve for the parameter \(t\).
  3. Compute the intersection point.
  4. Verify the point in both the line and the plane.
  5. Decide whether the line is perpendicular to the plane, parallel to it, or neither.
  6. Explain how the equation in \(t\) would look if the line were parallel to the plane.
  7. Prepare a labelled three-dimensional sketch or two views of the configuration.

Required record

Include the substitution equation, the parameter, the point, both verification steps and the geometric classification.

Verification

Compare the direction vector of the line with a normal vector of the plane.
