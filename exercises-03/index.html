Exercise Set 03

The set follows the division intended for the later student workbook:

  • Problems 01–10 — required practice: calculate, convert, draw and verify;
  • Problems 11–20 — developing work: compare representations, justify constructions, explain geometry and prepare a mathematical note.

Each problem has its own Markdown file. Separate editable solution.md files will be added only in the forkable student workbook.

Problems 01–10 — required practice

Problem 01 — Generating points of a line Use a parameter to generate points, test membership and connect the equation with a drawing.

Problem 02 — Line through two points Construct two parametric descriptions from the same pair of points and verify their equivalence.

Problem 03 — From a direction to a normal equation Convert one parametric line to normal and implicit forms and check generated points.

Problem 04 — Slope, direction and a vertical line Interpret slope geometrically and compare ordinary and vertical-line representations.

Problem 05 — Intersection of two lines Find one intersection algebraically, verify it and compare the result with the graph.

Problem 06 — Classifying pairs of lines Distinguish intersecting, parallel and coincident lines from their directions and equations.

Problem 07 — Distance from a point to a line Compute the distance, construct the perpendicular foot and verify orthogonality.

Problem 08 — Generating points of a plane Use two parameters to generate and test points of a plane.

Problem 09 — Normal vector of a plane Compute a normal with the cross product and derive a normal equation.

Problem 10 — Plane through three points Construct parametric and normal descriptions and verify all original points.

Problems 11–20 — developing work

Problem 11 — Many parametrizations of one line Document how different initial points and direction multiples describe the same set.

Problem 12 — The vertical line and its representations Compare parametric and normal descriptions with the limitation of slope-intercept form.

Problem 13 — Three descriptions of one line Prepare a compact presentation card connecting parametric, slope and normal forms.

Problem 14 — Two methods for one intersection Solve one intersection parametrically and in Cartesian form, then compare the methods.

Problem 15 — Why the perpendicular gives the shortest distance Combine numerical comparison, a general expression and a right-triangle argument.

Problem 16 — Points at a fixed distance from a line Derive and interpret the two parallel lines forming a fixed-distance locus.

Problem 17 — One direction or two? Compare line and plane generation using dependent and independent directions.

Problem 18 — Collinear and noncollinear triples Use dependence and the cross product to compare triples of points.

Problem 19 — Intersection of a line and a plane Find and classify a simple line-plane intersection and prepare a spatial visualization.

Problem 20 — Choosing a representation Build a verified decision guide for selecting parametric, slope or normal descriptions.
