Problem 10 — Intersections and inequalities

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 01 Lecture 01

Task

Consider the two lines

\[ y=x+1, \]

and

\[ y=-x+5. \]
  1. Find their intersection point.
  2. Check the obtained point in both equations.
  3. Draw both lines on one coordinate system.
  4. Shade the region described simultaneously by $$ y\ge x+1, $$ and $$ y\le -x+5. $$
  5. Decide whether each of the points $$ (0,2),\quad (2,3),\quad (3,3),\quad (1,5) $$ belongs to the shaded region.
  6. State which parts of the two boundary lines belong to the boundary of the region.

Required record

Include the algebraic intersection calculation, the graph with the shaded region, and a point-classification table.

Verification

For every classified point, show both inequality tests rather than relying only on the drawing.
