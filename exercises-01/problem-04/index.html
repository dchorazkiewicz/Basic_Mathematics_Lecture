Problem 04 — Distance from a right triangle

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 01 Lecture 01

Task

Let

\[ P=(-1,2),\qquad Q=(5,10). \]
  1. Plot \(P\) and \(Q\).
  2. Draw the horizontal and vertical sides of the right triangle whose hypotenuse is the segment \(PQ\).
  3. Compute the horizontal and vertical coordinate differences.
  4. Use the Pythagorean theorem to calculate \(d(P,Q)\).
  5. Repeat the calculation using the coordinate distance formula.
  6. Compute \(d(Q,P)\) and compare the result.

Required record

Include the labelled triangle, both coordinate differences and the complete distance calculation.

Verification

Your drawing must show legs of lengths \(6\) and \(8\). Check that the calculated hypotenuse is consistent with this triangle.
