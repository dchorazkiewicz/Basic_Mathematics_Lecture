Problem 17 — When do two vectors generate the plane?

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 02 Lecture 02

Problem

Consider the vectors

\[ a=(2,1),\qquad b=(4,2),\qquad c=(-1,2). \]
  1. Show that \(a\) and \(b\) are linearly dependent by finding a scalar relation between them.
  2. Draw several combinations \(\alpha a+\beta b\) and describe the set of endpoints obtained.
  3. For the pair \(a,c\), find coefficients generating the vectors \((1,0)\) and \((0,1)\).
  4. Use those results to explain why every vector \((x,y)\) can be generated from \(a\) and \(c\).
  5. Draw a small grid of combinations \(\alpha a+\beta c\) for integer coefficients \(-2\leq\alpha,\beta\leq2\).
  6. Compare the two diagrams and formulate a criterion, in geometric language, for when two plane vectors generate the whole plane.

Required record

Include both generating diagrams, the coefficient calculations for \((1,0)\) and \((0,1)\), and a concise criterion.

Verification

Substitute the coefficients found for \((1,0)\) and \((0,1)\) back into the corresponding linear combinations.
