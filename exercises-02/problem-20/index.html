Problem 20 — A complete note on vector decomposition

Damian Chorążkiewicz

← Exercise set 02 Lecture 02

Problem

A point starts at

\[ P=(1,2) \]

and undergoes the consecutive changes

\[ u=(4,2),\qquad v=(-1,3). \]

Let

\[ d=(1,1),\qquad e=(1,-1), \]

so that \(B=(d,e)\) is a basis of the plane.

Prepare a concise mathematical note containing the following steps.

  1. Draw the two-step route from \(P\) and find the final point.
  2. Compute the total displacement \(w=u+v.\)
  3. Compute \(\operatorname{proj}_d w\) and the perpendicular remainder.
  4. Verify the orthogonality of the remainder to \(d\).
  5. Find the coordinates \([w]_B\).
  6. Explain how the basis decomposition of \(w\) is related to the projection decomposition in this particular orthogonal basis.
  7. Reconstruct \(w\) from its \(B\)-coordinates.
  8. Draw on one diagram the route, total displacement, basis vectors and two decomposition components.
  9. End with a short explanation of how the same vector can simultaneously describe:
  10. total change;
  11. a sum of consecutive changes;
  12. a parallel and perpendicular decomposition;
  13. coordinates relative to a chosen basis.

Required record

The note must contain complete calculations, one annotated diagram, all verification steps and a conclusion of six to ten sentences.

Verification

Check independently that the route endpoint, the coordinate sum, the projection decomposition and the basis reconstruction all produce the same total displacement \(w\).
